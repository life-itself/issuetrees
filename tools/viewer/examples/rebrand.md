# Seeds of Renaissance Rebrand — 2026

## Objective

Define the Seeds of Renaissance brand before its public rollout and launch.

## Known outputs

- Brand narrative — marked complete in the current source plan.
- Brand design guidelines — identified as the next priority.
- Seeds of Renaissance manifesto — marked complete in the current source plan.
- Consumer funnel and user-flow map.

## Scope note

This project defines the brand. Applying the brand across public channels belongs to the [brand rollout](2606-seeds-of-renaissance-brand-rollout.md), while the public announcement and activation belong to the [launch project](2607-seeds-of-renaissance-launch.md).

The decision about whether the Over the Mountains podcast is renamed, and what its canonical name should be, belongs to this rebrand project. Applying that decision to the podcast feed, artwork, URLs, and public channels belongs to the brand rollout.

- What distribution platforms and channels do we have (e.g. YouTube, Apple Podcasts), and what sub-channels or "stations" sit within them? **✅ Like Nate Hagens: one platform, with one or more stations/series within it.**
  - What YouTube channel(s) do we have? **✅ One: Seeds of Renaissance — https://www.youtube.com/@SeedsOfRenaissance.**
  - What podcast(s) do we have? **✅ One podcast on the major platforms (Apple Podcasts, Spotify), called Seeds of Renaissance. Stations are distinguished within that single feed by title prefix and episode type, not by separate feeds; revisit a separate feed only once a station has its own proven audience (Rufus, 2026-09-25).**
  - What stations do we have (series that get episode numbers or a YouTube playlist)? **✅ Two or more: Over the Mountains for Rufus-led intellectual interviews; probably one for Temple of the Sacred World; possibly an extra one, "Making sense of", like Nate Hagens's Frankly.**
    - Is Seeds of Renaissance itself a station? **✅ No — umbrella brand only for now. Material outside the named stations goes out unnumbered on the main channel in topic playlists. If a recurring format emerges (e.g. Rufus–Sylvie conversations), make it a station then and name it for what it has become (Rufus, 2026-09-25).**
    - What is the Over the Mountains podcast called after the rebrand? **✅ It keeps the name Over the Mountains, as a station within Seeds of Renaissance: "Seeds of Renaissance presents Over the Mountains". It moves into the single SoR YouTube channel and podcast feed; overthemountains.org is to be set up (Rufus, 2026-09-25). Application is brand-rollout work.**
    - What are the name and editorial line of the Sylvie-led station? Working title Temple of the Sacred World; Sylvie owns the decision. Until it is settled, its pieces can go out as fast releases and be moved into the station once it launches.
    - Does "Making sense of" become a station, and what is its format?
    - How are stations presented? **✅ One SoR master kit (typeface, logo lockup, sonic sting) with a variant per station (colour, artwork, title card). "Seeds of Renaissance presents…" appears in the intro and on the episode cover/title image, not in written titles. Describe stations publicly by host and format; tone descriptors such as "masculine/feminine" stay internal (Rufus, 2026-09-25).**
    - What belongs in each station? **✅ Decided per piece by the station host using the triage rule in the [publication tracks](2602-content-delivery-pipeline/publication-tracks.md); if in doubt it goes out as a fast release.**
  - What are our social media handles? **✅ Instagram: @seedsofrenaissance; Facebook: Seeds of Renaissance; X/Twitter: @SeedRenaissance; LinkedIn: seedsofrenaissance; Linktree: @seedsofrenaissance; YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/@SeedsOfRenaissance.**
  - How do the website, YouTube playlists, podcast feed and newsletter relate? Partial direction: the website is the canonical home for numbered episodes; fast releases live on YouTube unless promoted.
  - What happens to the Over the Mountains Substack? Current direction (Rufus, 2026-09-25): move off Substack, distributing the podcast through Riverside (probable) and YouTube, and switching to our own newsletter. The switchover itself still needs planning.

The critique
and the refinements

Beautiful at a glance can become unreadable at scale. A complete project tree will shrink beyond usefulness if fitted to one screen. Start at a readable zoom with deeper branches collapsed; always offer “Fit tree” and an explicit zoom readout.

Answers can dominate the inquiry. Keep the question above its branch baseline, the answer below it, and preview long answers in three lines. Expanding an answer changes height, never the reading measure.

Colour alone cannot explain state. Reserve it for branch families. The word “Answer” and the source checkmark provide meaning; unanswered questions need no red flag or implied failure.

The canvas cannot become a navigation puzzle. Keep opening and reset controls visible, show a brief pan/zoom hint, support keyboard interaction, and retain a readable outline alternative on narrow screens.
